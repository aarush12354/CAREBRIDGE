# CAREBRIDGE

**Bridging the gap between diagnosis and treatment.**

CAREBRIDGE is a patient-support platform designed to help clinics and hospitals continue supporting patients after diagnosis.

The platform brings together treatment reminders, appointments, diagnostic tests, medicine tracking, medicine delivery, finance guidance, support requests, and health-awareness campaigns in one clean and easy-to-use experience.

---

## Project Overview

Getting diagnosed is only the first step in a patient's healthcare journey.

Many patients do not continue with the recommended treatment because:

- They forget follow-up appointments or tests
- Medicine schedules become difficult to manage
- Treatment costs may feel unaffordable
- They have small treatment-related questions but do not want to book another full appointment
- They are unsure about what needs to happen next

CAREBRIDGE is designed to reduce these gaps by helping patients stay connected to their treatment journey.

---

## Key Features

### Patient Treatment Journey

Patients can view their treatment journey in one place, including:

- Upcoming appointments
- Diagnostic tests
- Follow-up visits
- Recommended health checkups
- Treatment-related next steps

### Medicine Tracking

Patients can:

- View a time-based medicine intake chart
- Mark medicines as taken
- Track medicine adherence
- Receive refill reminders
- Request medicine delivery

### Medicine Delivery

CAREBRIDGE can support a clinic-connected medicine delivery workflow where patients can request prescribed medicines directly through the platform.

### Treatment Support

Patients can request help for smaller treatment-related questions through:

- Trained clinic executives
- Medical health experts
- Callback support

CAREBRIDGE does not replace doctors, diagnosis, or emergency medical care.

### Finance Support

Patients can explore available financial options such as:

- Health insurance
- EMI facilities
- Healthcare financing

Eligibility and approval remain subject to the respective insurance or finance provider.

### Health Awareness

The platform can also support health-awareness campaigns and interactive sessions covering topics such as:

- Women's health
- Menstrual health
- Health after menopause
- Structural deformities
- Preventive healthcare
- Regular health checkups

### Clinic Dashboard

Clinics and hospitals can monitor:

- Active patients
- Upcoming follow-ups
- Missed actions
- Support requests
- Finance requests
- Medicine refill requests
- Patient treatment progress

---

## Business Model

CAREBRIDGE follows a **B2B2C model**.

Clinics and hospitals can offer the platform to their patients through:

- Subscription-based access
- Implementation / purchase model
- Optional patient-support executive

The planned minimum subscription period is **six months**.

## Technology

This project is built using:

- HTML5
- CSS3
- Vanilla JavaScript
